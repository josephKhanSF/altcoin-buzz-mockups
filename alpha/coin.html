Every mark is one coin we can measure. The lane has no up and no down: moving right means growing faster than other coins, not that the price is rising. Market Rank, three blocks down, lines these coins up by their scores; this lane lines them up by growth in use, and the same coin can sit differently on the two.

A PLACE, NOT A DIRECTION

A high place can mean falling less in a falling market. This lane orders coins against each other. It never points up or down, and we never turn it into a price call.

What this says

Coins whose use has been growing faster than the pack have tended to do better than coins whose use is slowing, over weeks to months, and only relative to each other.

If you are choosing between two coins, the one further right on the lane has the better recent evidence. That is the whole claim. It is at its most useful when narrowing a long list to a short one.

What this does not say

It does not say HYPE will rise, and it does not time an entry. The whole market can fall together and take every coin on the lane with it.

The evidence behind it is real but modest, most of it is from before 2023, and we are still testing it against our own board. When this block and the chart disagree, that is a question to ask, not a signal to trade.
