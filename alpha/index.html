    Band colours mean bands, read per coin: bullish, neutral, bearish. The averages and shelf means printed on this page are plain arithmetic means of member coins' real scores, drawn so shelves can be compared on one ruler. They are not a sector score: no sector is banded, no group is given a dial, and the dial belongs to a coin's TNT score alone. A coin can hold two sectors, so the rows do not sum to the board. Decoupling is shown here as position, never as colour: a mark above the parity line is a chart running ahead of the token you can buy.
